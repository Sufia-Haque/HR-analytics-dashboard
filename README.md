# HR-analytics-dashboard
HR Analytics Dashboard built in Power BI analyzing employee attrition across 1,480 employees

---

## Project Overview

This project presents a **2-page interactive HR Analytics Dashboard** built in Microsoft Power BI, analyzing employee attrition patterns across a workforce of **1,480 employees**. Using real-world HR data, the dashboard answers critical business questions that every HR team and company leadership needs to address — *who is leaving, why, and what can we do about it?*

> **Business Objective:** Identify key attrition drivers across departments, job roles, age groups, and satisfaction levels — and deliver actionable recommendations to reduce employee turnover.

## 🔑 Key Findings

### 1. 📦 Department Risk
- **Research & Development** has the highest attrition count
- These are the most skilled and expensive employees to replace
- Immediate retention intervention is critical for R&D talent

### 2. 👤 Job Role Risk
- **Laboratory Technicians** — highest attrition (60+ employees)
- **Sales Executives** — second highest (~57 employees)
- **Research Scientists** — third highest (~47 employees)
- Senior roles (Manager, Research Director) show very low attrition

### 3. 🎯 Age Group Risk
- **26–35 age group** has the highest attrition — RED flag
- These are ambitious mid-career professionals with many external opportunities
- **55+ age group** is the most stable — GREEN signal

### 4. ✈️ Business Travel Impact
- **70.95%** of attrition comes from employees who Travel Rarely (largest group)
- **Travel Frequently** employees show disproportionately high attrition relative to their headcount
- Frequent travel creates burnout and reduces work-life balance

### 5. 💰 Compensation vs Retention
- Clear salary progression from Level 1 (~$2,700) to Level 5 (~$19,500)
- Despite progressive pay structure, lower job levels still show highest attrition
- Salary alone does not guarantee retention — growth and culture matter equally

### 6. 😔 Job Satisfaction Signal
- Employees with **Level 1 satisfaction (Very Dissatisfied)** show highest attrition
- Even Level 3–4 satisfied employees leave, suggesting engagement beyond satisfaction is needed

---

## 💡 Strategic Recommendations

| # | Recommendation | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Launch dedicated R&D retention program with stay interviews and career ladders | 🔴 Critical |
| 02 | Create fast-track growth pathways for 26–35 age group with clear promotion timelines | 🔴 Critical |
| 03 | Cap mandatory travel frequency — introduce remote-first meeting policies | 🟠 High |
| 04 | Flag Level 1 satisfaction employees for immediate manager 1-on-1 intervention | 🟠 High |
| 05 | Strengthen entry-level (Job Level 1–2) onboarding with 90-day check-ins and peer buddies | 🟡 Medium |
